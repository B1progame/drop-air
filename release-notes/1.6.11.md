# Drop Air 1.6.11

Fixes stale dashboard links after Drop Air has been hidden in the system tray.

- Resuming the desktop window explicitly refreshes the session link, QR code, incoming files, and shared text.
- A signed, HTTP-only recovery cookie lets an already-authorized page renew its session key after long hidden periods suspend its browser timers.
- The recovery secret persists in Drop Air's data directory, so normal and portable installs preserve recovery across app restarts.
